Arcane Glass Buttons

Every button outside the character sheets and the card face. Dark glass at rest; on hover the button’s own colour fills up from the bottom and the text takes the other colour. Players confirm in Hope and back out in Fear. The GM confirms in Fear and backs out in Hope.

Why it flips. A player who backs out does it out of fear, so their cancel is Fear. When the GM backs off, the players get some breathing room, so the GM’s cancel is Hope. Each side’s confirm is their own resource’s colour. One exception: when the GM answers something a player asked for, Allow is Hope and Decline is Fear, because that is what the answer means for the table.

The numbers

Size
44 px tall (34 small, 54 large), 18 px side padding, 12 px radius (10 small, round for icon-only close). Icon-only buttons are 44 × 44.
Type
IBM Plex Sans 600, 14 px (12.5 small, 17 large). No capitals, no tracking.
Rest
Dark glass rgba(13,20,23,.86) with the role colour rising from the bottom (38% for confirm, 22% for cancel), a 1 px rim in the role colour and a soft 14 px inner glow.
Hover / focus
A fill layer scales up from the bottom over 260 ms (cubic-bezier(.3,.7,.3,1)); text turns to the other colour over 220 ms. Gold fill #f0c873 → #c8953f with deep-purple text #2f2357. Purple fill #5b48a3 → #3b2d6b with gold text #f3cf86. Ember fill #c8503c → #8a2a1e with gold text. Neutral fill slate #2c3c42 → #1f2b30 with gold text. Keyboard focus shows the same fill plus a 2 px gold outline, 3 px offset.
Press
scale(.96), 150 ms.
Switched on
aria-pressed="true": gold glass at rest, gold rim. Roll toggle: Normal switches on in pale grey glass; Advantage and Disadvantage show their colour as text when off and as full glass when on (Hope / Fear for players, Fear / Hope for the GM).
Disabled
Grey glass, grey text, no fill, no press. Put the reason next to it (“Needs 1 Hope”).
Reduced motion
The fill and colour change happen instantly.
Out of scope
Character sheets and the card face (.hc-face) keep their own buttons.

CSS to drop in

Put data-viewer="player" or data-viewer="gm" on the combat page root, then give each button class="btn" and a data-role of confirm, cancel, neutral, tool, danger, or allow / deny for the GM’s answer to a player’s request; the roll toggle uses normal, advantage and disadvantage with aria-pressed. Sizes: add sm, lg, icon, round.


    

    
In React this is one <Button role="confirm" | "cancel" | "neutral" | "tool" | "danger" | "allow" | "deny" | "normal" | "advantage" | "disadvantage"> that renders <button className="btn" data-role={role}>; the viewer comes from the page, not from each button.
